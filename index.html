Capabilities

Four systems. One agent.

Every trade Quantra makes passes through the same core loop, running continuously across every market it watches.

Market scanning

Continuously reads order books and price action across multiple pairs, flagging shifts in volume and volatility as they happen.

Signal generation

Turns raw market data into ranked, confidence-scored signals, filtering out noise before a single trade is considered.

Autonomous execution

Places and manages trades on-chain directly, with no manual approval step standing between signal and action.

Risk management

Sizes every position against account exposure and market conditions, and stands down when conditions turn unclear.

How it works

One loop, repeated every cycle.

  1. 01

    Perceive

    Ingests live price feeds, liquidity data and on-chain activity across every market it's assigned to watch.

  2. 02

    Decide

    Weighs each opportunity against its risk model and current exposure, and scores its confidence in the move.

  3. 03

    Execute

    Sends the trade on-chain the moment confidence clears its threshold — no queue, no delay.

  4. 04

    Report

    Logs the reasoning and outcome publicly, so every decision the agent makes stays auditable.
